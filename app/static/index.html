Coombe & McCarthy (2000) Double-Sigmoid Growth Modeler

Reconstructs multi-temporal grape berry growth curves (GrapeCS-ML Datasets 1–4) separated by the herbaceous lag phase.

V(t) = v0 + v1 / (1 + e^-k1(t-t1)) + v2 / (1 + e^-k2(t-t2))

Autonomous Winter Cane Pruning & Robotic Harvesting Localization

Translates Xu et al. (2014) blue-channel branch binarization + skeletonization and Luo et al. (2016) peduncle cutting point detection (<0.09s latency).

Pruning Robotics Pipeline

  1. Blue-Channel Isolation: Woody cane branches exhibit peak contrast against soil and winter sky in the blue spectrum.
  2. Otsu Binarization: Separates foreground woody canes from cluttered background trellises.
  3. Morphological Skeletonization: Reduces canes to 1-pixel topological medial axis skeleton.
  4. Harris Corner Detection: Pinpoints bud nodes and branch bifurcations for robotic shears.
  5. Luo et al. Cutting Point: Computes coordinates 25px above cluster shoulder in <0.09s.
